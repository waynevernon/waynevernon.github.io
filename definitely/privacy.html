Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Definitely is designed to keep your data on your devices and under your control.

What the app stores

Your vocabulary, flashcard progress, and preferences live on your device and sync to your iCloud account so you can back up and restore across your devices. We don’t run user accounts, and we never store your words on our own servers.

Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app and to improve it. PostHog receives a random device identifier and high-level usage events—things like how many words you add or delete, which settings you toggle, your notification schedule choices, purchase attempts, and basic error logs.

We don’t send any of your saved words, definitions, or sentences. PostHog’s hosting region and data-retention rules apply to anything they receive.

Suggestions & Definitions

When “Suggest Words & Definitions” is enabled, the words you type are sent directly from your device to datamuse.com (autocomplete) and dictionaryapi.dev (definitions). We don’t see or store those requests.

Notifications

All notifications are created on your device. If you enable reminders, the app uses your stored words locally to build those notifications. No push tokens or content are sent to our servers.

Purchases

All purchases run through Apple using StoreKit. We log non-sensitive purchase events to PostHog—product ID, price, and transaction status/ID—so we can track reliability. Apple handles all payment details.

Support emails

If you email support from inside the app, the message includes your PostHog device ID, the app version, and your iOS version. This helps us troubleshoot issues.

Children’s privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.
