Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Definitely stores your vocabulary, flashcard progress, and preferences on your device and syncs them to your iCloud account so you can back up and restore across your devices. We don’t run user accounts or keep your words on our own servers.

We use PostHog Cloud analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. PostHog receives a random device identifier plus high-level app events (e.g., words added/deleted counts, settings toggles, notification schedule choices, purchase attempts/outcomes, error logs). We don’t send your saved words, definitions, or sentences to PostHog. Hosting region and retention follow PostHog Cloud’s policies.

When you enable “Suggest Words & Definitions,” the words you type are sent to dictionaryapi.dev (for definitions) and datamuse.com (for autocomplete suggestions). Those requests go straight from your device to those services; we don’t store them.

Notifications stay on your device. If you turn on Word of the Day or Flashcard reminders, the app uses your stored words to compose notifications; no push tokens are sent to our servers.

Purchases are handled by Apple via StoreKit. We log non-sensitive purchase events to PostHog (product ID, price, transaction status/ID) for reliability tracking; payment details stay with Apple.

If you email support from the app, the message includes your PostHog device ID, app version, and iOS version to help troubleshoot.

This app is not intended for children under 13.
