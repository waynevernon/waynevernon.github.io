Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This app doesn’t collect personal information—everything you enter stays anonymous.

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app and make it better. Some of the words you type may be sent to third-party services to provide autocomplete and dictionary suggestions.

Your app data may be backed up to your iCloud account so you can restore it if needed. We don’t share your data with anyone else.

This app is not intended for children under 13.
